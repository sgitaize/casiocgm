Casio-style LCD watchface for Pebble Time 2 with live Nightscout CGM (blood glucose). As a diabetic you want a cool watchface too – that's why CasioCGM exists.

- Glucose value with trend arrow in the classic 5-digit box, coloured by your low/high thresholds (mg/dL or mmol/L)
- Smart sync: fetches right after each new sensor reading (reading time + interval + 30 s, Nightscout server time) – no waiting minutes for a fresh value
- Clear status: NOCON / NO-BG / OLDBG when data is missing or old
- Optional vibration on low / high (10 min cooldown)
- Alternative complications: steps, heart rate, weather, battery, second date – shake to cycle
- Editable labels, colours, seconds, date format, weekday language
- Configuration page with live preview

Open source: https://github.com/sgitaize/casiocgm
Design inspired by Quartz by Dalpek: https://apps.repebble.com/quartz-by-dalpek_31cfe29ecd814df4b5ca8bb4
